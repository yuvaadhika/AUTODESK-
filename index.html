STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING & BIM
RCC FRAMEWORK & DETAILS

Structural Modeling & Detailing (BIM 2D/3D)

  • Structural Grid: 8,000 × 8,000 mm column bay with post-tensioned flat slabs (175mm thickness) + drop panels.
  • Columns (C1): 600 × 600 mm M40 concrete reinforced with 12-T25 high ductility bars and 8mm confinement ties.
  • Beams (B1): 300 × 600 mm M35 concrete with continuous top and bottom steel designed for ductile seismic shear.
  • Stairs & Slabs: Doglegged fire-escape stairwells with 150mm waist slabs and 2-hour fire endurance.
  • Finishes & Detailing: 600×600mm vitrified tile flooring over acoustic underlay and multi-layer waterproofing.
