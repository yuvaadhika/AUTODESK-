# AURA VERTICALIS — Sustainable B+G+9 Mixed-Use BIM Development
> **Centrally Located Biophilic & Climate-Responsive Mixed-Use Architectural Development**

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview
**AURA VERTICALIS** is a forward-thinking, centrally located **B+G+9 (11 Total Levels)** mixed-use urban development. The project harmoniously synthesizes active commercial vitality on the lower levels (Ground & 1F) with a 48-unit nature-integrated luxury residential sanctuary above (2F–9F), founded upon an automated basement parking hub with 24 fast EV charging bays.

- **Total Building Height:** 38,700 mm (+34,900 mm Above Ground Level)
- **Plot Dimensions (Assumed mm):** 48,000 mm (W) × 36,000 mm (D) = **1,728 m² GFA Envelope**
- **Building Footprint:** 40,000 mm × 30,000 mm = **1,200 m²**
- **Total Gross Built-Up Area (BUA):** **12,850 m²**
- **Proposed Structural Grid:** 8,000 mm × 8,000 mm Reinforced Concrete Frame (IS 456:2000 / IS 13920:2016)
- **Target Energy Reduction:** **62.8 kWh/m²/yr (-51% vs Standard Baseline)**

## 🌿 Core Architectural & Environmental Concepts

### 1. Breathable Central Courtyard (Ground to Roof)
- Open-to-sky **16,000 mm × 14,000 mm (224 m²)** central landscaped atrium extending continuously from Ground to Roof.
- Harnesses natural convective stack pressure to pull cool air through shaded ground walkways across reflection ponds, venting warm stale air through the rooftop.
- Target **4.5 air changes per hour (ACH)** passive airflow serving all 48 residential apartments.

### 2. Climate-Responsive Modular Façade System
- **External Shading Louvers:** Powder-coated aluminum louvers set at 42° to block 85% of peak direct infrared radiation while reflecting diffuse daylight.
- **Recessed Windows:** Deep reveal window placements to cut glare and eliminate direct solar heat gain.
- **Green Balconies:** Continuous cantilevered balconies (**1,800 mm projection**) with automated drip-irrigated native planters for 4.2°C microclimate cooling.
- **Operable Ventilation Openings:** Dual-aspect acoustic glazing for natural cross-ventilation.

### 3. Proposed Structural System & Detailing (IS 456 & IS 13920)
- **Grid:** 8,000 × 8,000 mm column bays with post-tensioned two-way flat slabs (175 mm depth) and drop panels (2500 × 2500 × 75 mm).
- **Proposed Columns (C1):** 600 × 600 mm Grade M40 concrete reinforced with 12-T25 rebar and 8mm ductile confinement ties with 135° seismic hooks.
- **Proposed Beams (B1):** 300 × 600 mm Grade M35 concrete with Top 3-T20 & Bottom 4-T25 rebar.
- **Finishes & Waterproofing:** 600 × 600 mm vitrified tiles over acoustic underlay (ΔLw = 21 dB) and elastomeric membrane waterproofing.

### 4. Conceptual Environmental Analysis (Autodesk Forma / Insight)
- **Target Spatial Daylight Autonomy (sDA > 300 lx):** 82.4% (LEED Platinum benchmark).
- **Target Energy Use Intensity (EUI):** 62.8 kWh/m²/yr (51% energy savings).
- **Clean Solar Generation:** 148 kWp bifacial BIPV yielding ~185,000 kWh/year.
- **Target Embodied Carbon:** 310 kgCO₂e/m² (-38% reduction via 40% GGBS slag concrete substitution).
